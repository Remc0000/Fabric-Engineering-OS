# Community Roadmap

This roadmap is governed by the [Fabric Engineering OS Constitution](../CONSTITUTION.md) and complements the canonical root [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).

Roadmap items express accepted direction. They are not date, staffing, release, or production commitments.

## Now: v0.9.0 preview

- Make bootstrap paths immediately usable for common Fabric scenarios.
- Establish concise contributor, maintainer, release, and adopter navigation.
- Validate Fabric-only source precedence and human approval boundaries.
- Exercise repository controls and DEV/TEST evidence paths.

## Next: preview validation

- Validate templates against representative Fabric projects and current Microsoft Learn guidance.
- Improve traceability among issues, decisions, tests, and operational evidence.
- Add compatibility and freshness metadata where maintainers confirm value.
- Refine guidance from adopter feedback without copying upstream content.

## Later: governed baseline

- Stabilize document contracts and navigation for v1.0.
- Publish reviewed extension and deprecation practices.
- Demonstrate that approval, merge, release, and production boundaries work in practice.

## How direction changes

1. Capture a possibility in the [Ideas Backlog](IDEAS-BACKLOG.md).
2. Open an issue with outcome, evidence, owner, scope, risks, and dependencies.
3. Obtain maintainer and required domain-owner acceptance.
4. Move accepted direction here; track implementation in issues and pull requests.
5. Record delivered behavior in release notes, not as a roadmap claim.

Human maintainers decide roadmap acceptance. Human release owners decide releases. Human production owners decide and perform production actions.
